The final recording. Deep night, lamp on, curtain slightly parted behind her with the orange of a streetlight through it. Hair loose, a plain navy long-sleeve top, Arjun's grey zip jacket thrown over her shoulders, too big for her. She is calm in the way of someone past fear. Her phone on the desk buzzes twice during the take; she does not look at it.

(23-30) Her eyes flick to the window and back. "He's outside right now. I saw his car from the balcony. He's texting that he just wants to talk. Arjun's at his brother's. N. isn't picking up." A breath. "If something happens to me, it's Rahul. Rahul Nair. It was always..." The clip cuts mid-word to black. End.
