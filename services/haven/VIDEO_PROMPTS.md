# Haven video prompts -- the nine that matter, 30 seconds each

Generator limit is 30 s per clip. Each clip below is one continuous webcam
take. The spoken lines are condensed from the Haven transcripts and keep
every clue; when the videos exist, `entries.transcript` and
`duration_seconds` for these nine get replaced with the spoken lines so the
text and the video agree (`npm run sync:transcripts`, to be added).

Export: MP4, H.264, 1080p, 16:9, no music, no captions. File names are the
existing `video_url` values (e.g. `2024-08-02.mp4`). Drop them into
`services/haven/public/videos/` and rebuild `haven`.

## Base block (paste at the start of every prompt)

Laptop webcam recording, 16:9, 1080p, slightly soft with mild sensor noise and auto-exposure, one continuous take, no cuts, no music, faint ceiling-fan room tone, realistic lip-sync and natural micro-movements, no captions, no logo, no text other than the timestamp. A young Indian woman in her late twenties, matching the reference photo for face and hair, sits at a small bedroom desk framed chest-up, camera slightly below eye level like a built-in laptop camera. Behind her, out of focus: a bed with a rumpled blanket, a wardrobe door, a curtain. She speaks Indian English with a Bengaluru accent, low voice, real pauses.
